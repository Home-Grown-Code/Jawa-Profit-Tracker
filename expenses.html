Expense Types

What counts as an expense?

Expenses are costs you paid that are not a physical part you sell. Common examples:

  • Shipping / postage on orders
  • Sales tax or VAT charged to you
  • Platform fees (eBay, Mercari, etc.)
  • Camera / photography costs
  • Packaging materials and boxes
  • Platform listing or final value fees
