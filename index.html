How it works

Three steps to track your profit

1

Log a purchase

Enter the item, what you paid, how many PCs (pieces) you bought, and the price you plan to sell each one for.

2

Sell individually

Update the Sheet tab as you sell pieces. PCs remaining, revenue and profit recalculate automatically.

3

Sync to your sheet

Every entry lands as a row in your own Google Sheet — no shared passwords, just your service account as Editor.

Your sheet lives in Google Sheets

Columns tracked: Item, Store, Total Cost, Qty PCs, Cost per PC, Sell Price per PC, PCs Sold, PCs Remaining, Revenue, Profit, Status, Notes. Edit anything on the Sheet tab and it writes straight back to your spreadsheet.

One-time setup: create a Google Sheet, share it with the tracker's service account (Editor), enter the Sheet ID in the backend .env / Render variables, and deploy. See Guide.
